How it works

  • Tap a ring or a voice key to select it. On the selected ring, tap a step to cycle tak, dum, ghost, off.
  • Long-press (or Shift-click) a tak or dum to accent it.
  • The arrows rotate the selected loop against the others. Loop sets its length; Mute silences it.
  • Drag a dial up or down, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Tap the LCD for the next pattern.
  • The two small meters show beat and bar gravity. Flat bars mean no felt downbeat.
